What Kind of International Insurance Do You Need?

The right type of coverage depends on where you are traveling, how long you will be away, your home country, your destination, and what you want the insurance to protect.

IMG offers several categories of international insurance. Instead of assuming that one plan is right for every traveler, it is important to first understand the purpose of each type of coverage.

UNDERSTAND THE DIFFERENCE

Travel Medical, Travel Protection & International Health

These types of insurance may sound similar, but they are designed for different needs.

Travel Medical Insurance

Temporary medical coverage for eligible travelers who become sick or injured while traveling outside their home country.

Depending on the plan, coverage may include eligible medical expenses and emergency medical evacuation benefits.

Travel Protection

Travel protection products may help protect against certain covered financial losses connected with a trip.

Depending on the plan, covered events may involve trip cancellation, trip interruption, travel delay, baggage, medical expenses, or other specified travel-related losses.

International Health Insurance

International health insurance is designed for certain people who live or work internationally and need longer-term private medical coverage.

These plans are different from temporary travel medical insurance.

COMMON SITUATIONS

Who May Want to Explore International Insurance?

IMG offers products for several types of international travelers. Eligibility and available plans depend on the individual situation and the applicable product.

Visitors to the United States

Family members, parents, relatives, or other eligible international visitors coming to the United States may want to explore temporary travel medical insurance.

Traveling Outside Your Home Country

International vacationers and business travelers may want to understand what medical coverage they have while outside their home country and whether additional travel medical insurance is appropriate.

Students & Exchange Visitors

International students, scholars, and exchange participants may have insurance requirements or medical coverage needs while studying or participating in a program abroad.

Longer-Term International Residents

Expats and certain global citizens living or working internationally may need to consider longer-term international health insurance rather than temporary travel medical insurance.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Travel Insurance Is Not ACA Marketplace Coverage

Travel medical insurance and international insurance products are separate from ACA Marketplace health insurance.

These products should not be assumed to provide the same benefits, protections, eligibility rules, or coverage structure as an ACA Marketplace major medical plan.

If you are a Texas resident looking for regular individual or family health insurance rather than travel-related coverage, visit my Texas Health Insurance or ACA / Obamacare pages.

BEFORE YOU BUY

What Should You Compare?

Do not choose an international insurance plan based only on price. Review the actual plan documents and consider the benefits and limitations that matter for your trip or international situation.

Medical Benefits

Review eligible medical benefits, maximum limits, deductibles, and other cost-sharing provisions.

Emergency Evacuation

Review whether emergency medical evacuation benefits are included and the conditions that apply.

Trip Protection Benefits

If trip cancellation, interruption, delay, or baggage protection is important to you, review the specific covered reasons and benefit limits.

Pre-Existing Conditions

Review how the specific plan treats pre-existing conditions and whether any applicable benefit, limitation, exclusion, or eligibility requirement applies.

Destination & Eligibility

Plan availability can depend on factors such as home country, destination, age, trip duration, residency, and other eligibility requirements.

Exclusions & Limitations

Read the certificate, policy, or other governing plan documents so you understand exclusions, limitations, requirements, and covered benefits.
