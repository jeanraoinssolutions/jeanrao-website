Obamacare / ACA

What is Obamacare?

"Obamacare" is the term commonly used for health insurance associated with the Affordable Care Act, often abbreviated as ACA.

The ACA Marketplace is one of the major ways individuals and families can obtain health insurance. Premiums, subsidy eligibility, benefits, deductibles, maximum out-of-pocket amounts, provider networks, and other terms can vary depending on your circumstances and the plan you choose.

If you live in Texas and are considering Obamacare, ACA Marketplace coverage, or other health insurance, I can help you understand the available options before you decide what to do next.

Other Health Insurance Options

Are there options besides ACA (Obamacare)?

For some eligible Texas residents, there may be private health insurance options outside the ACA Marketplace.

Different health insurance plans can have significant differences in eligibility requirements, premiums, benefits, provider networks, maximum out-of-pocket amounts, exclusions, limitations, and other contract terms.

Before choosing health insurance, it is important to compare the actual benefits and requirements of the plans available to you.
