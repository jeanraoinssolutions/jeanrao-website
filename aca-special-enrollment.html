Special Enrollment Period (SEP)

Missed Open Enrollment? You may still be eligible to sign up for or change health insurance if you experienced a Qualifying Life Event (QLE).

The 60-Day Rule

In most cases, you have 60 days from the date of your Qualifying Life Event to enroll in a new plan. If you know a major change is coming (like losing job-based coverage), you can often apply up to 60 days in advance.

Qualifying Life Events (QLE)

Category Qualifying Events
Loss of Coverage Losing job-based health insurance, losing Medicaid/CHIP eligibility, or aging off a parent's plan at age 26.
Household Changes Getting married, having a baby, adopting a child, or getting divorced/separated.
Relocation Moving to a new ZIP code or county that offers different health insurance plan options.
Income & Status Changes Changes in household income that alter subsidy eligibility, gaining citizenship, or release from incarceration.

Verification & Documentation

HealthCare.gov requires proof of your Qualifying Life Event (such as a notice of coverage loss, marriage certificate, or proof of new address). Coverage typically begins the first day of the month following plan selection.
