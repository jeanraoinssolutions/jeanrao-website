Understanding ACA Plan Tiers in Texas

ACA Marketplace plans are categorized into metal categories—Bronze, Silver, and Gold. The metal level indicates how you and your plan split the costs of covered health care services.

Texas Coverage Gap Warning (Under 100% FPL)

Because Texas has not expanded Medicaid, adults earning below 100% of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL) generally do not qualify for HealthCare.gov subsidies or Medicaid. Marketplace subsidy eligibility in Texas begins at 100% FPL ($15,960/yr for individuals in 2026).

2026 Maximum Out-of-Pocket Limits

No matter which metal tier you choose, all ACA-compliant plans set an annual cap on covered in-network costs. For 2026, the maximum out-of-pocket limit is $10,600 for individual plans and $21,200 for family plans.
