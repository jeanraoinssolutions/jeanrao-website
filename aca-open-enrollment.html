When Is ACA Open Enrollment?

For Marketplace health insurance, Open Enrollment runs from November 1 through January 15 each year.

During this period, you can apply for Marketplace coverage, compare available plans, and enroll in coverage for the upcoming year.

UNDERSTANDING OPEN ENROLLMENT

What Is ACA Open Enrollment?

Open Enrollment is the annual period when people can enroll in a Marketplace health insurance plan.

It is also an opportunity for people who already have Marketplace coverage to review their current plan, update their household and income information, and compare plans for the new year.

Your health care needs, household, income, available plans, and costs may change from one year to the next. Reviewing your options can help you choose coverage that better fits your situation.

ALREADY HAVE ACA COVERAGE?

Don't Automatically Assume Your Current Plan Is Best

If you already have Marketplace coverage, Open Enrollment is a good time to review your information and compare available plans.

Update Your Income

Marketplace savings are based on your expected household income for the year you are applying for coverage. If your income changes, your potential savings may change as well.

Update Your Household

Changes in your household can affect your Marketplace application and potential financial assistance.

Compare Plans

Plans and prices can change from year to year. Review available plans instead of assuming your current plan will remain the best choice.

Check Your Network

If you regularly see particular doctors, specialists, or hospitals, check whether they participate in the plan you are considering.

AFTER OPEN ENROLLMENT

What If You Miss January 15?

After January 15, you generally cannot enroll in or change Marketplace coverage unless you qualify for a Special Enrollment Period.

Certain life events may create a Special Enrollment Period. Examples can include losing other health coverage, getting married, having a baby, or moving.

Your eligibility depends on your specific circumstances and the applicable Marketplace rules.

LOST EMPLOYER HEALTH INSURANCE?

You May Not Have to Wait Until Open Enrollment

Losing job-based health coverage may qualify you for a Special Enrollment Period. This can allow you to enroll in Marketplace coverage outside the regular Open Enrollment period.

If you recently lost your employer health insurance, don't assume that January 15 is your only opportunity to get Marketplace coverage.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Information You May Want to Have Ready

Household Information

Information about the people in your household and who will be included on your application.

Expected Income

An estimate of your household income for the year you want coverage. Marketplace savings are based on expected income.

Current Coverage

Information about current or recently lost health insurance, including employer coverage.

Doctors & Hospitals

If you have preferred doctors, specialists, hospitals, or medications, consider checking plan networks and formularies when comparing plans.
