ACA Open Enrollment: November 1 – January 15

Learn when you can enroll, modify your plan, or secure coverage for the upcoming plan year through the HealthCare.gov Marketplace.

Important Enrollment Deadlines

Deadline What Happens
November 1 Open Enrollment begins. You can apply, compare plans, and select coverage.
December 15 Last day to enroll or change plans for coverage starting January 1.
January 15 Open Enrollment ends. Last day to sign up for the plan year.
February 1 Coverage starts for enrollments submitted between December 16 and January 15.

Missed Open Enrollment?

After January 15, you can generally only enroll in or change Marketplace coverage if you qualify for a Special Enrollment Period (SEP) due to a life event (such as getting married, moving, losing other coverage, or having a baby).
