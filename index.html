HEALTH INSURANCE OPTIONS

Which Health Insurance Option Fits Your Situation?

ACA Marketplace coverage is one important option, but it is not the only type of health insurance available. Your situation, timing, income, health needs, and access to other coverage can affect which options are worth considering.

ACA Marketplace

Individual and family health insurance available through the Marketplace, with financial assistance available to eligible applicants.

Employer Coverage

Employer-sponsored health insurance may be an important option when available and affordable.

COBRA

Some people who lose employer coverage may consider continuing their existing employer plan through COBRA.

Private Health Insurance

Certain private health insurance options are available outside the ACA Marketplace, subject to eligibility, plan availability, and policy terms.
