Health Insurance Option

Looking for Health Coverage Outside the ACA Marketplace?

I also offer access to a private health insurance option from Presidio Health Insurance Company for eligible Texas residents.

This coverage is not an ACA Marketplace plan. Presidio describes its plans as private health insurance regulated by the Texas Department of Insurance. Specific benefits, eligibility, costs, exclusions, limitations, and policy terms vary by plan and individual circumstances.

Who May Want to Learn More

  • Individuals or families looking for alternatives to ACA Marketplace coverage
  • College students and young adults
  • People between jobs or who have recently lost employer-sponsored health coverage
  • Individuals who want to compare different health insurance options

Some Plan Features

  • $0 annual deductible on the plan shown by Presidio
  • 50% coinsurance, with plan benefits changing after the applicable maximum out-of-pocket amount is reached
  • Nationwide provider access through the plan's primary network
  • No annual or lifetime policy maximums, subject to applicable benefit limitations
  • Available outside the traditional ACA Open Enrollment period, subject to eligibility and plan availability

Eligibility, availability, premiums, benefits, out-of-pocket amounts, exclusions, limitations, and other policy terms vary. Please review the applicable insurance documents carefully before enrolling.
