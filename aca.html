What Is ACA / Obamacare?

The Affordable Care Act (ACA), often called "Obamacare," created the Health Insurance Marketplace where eligible individuals and families can shop for health insurance coverage.

Depending on your household circumstances, income, and other eligibility factors, you may qualify for financial assistance that can reduce the cost of Marketplace coverage.

Understanding the basic rules before choosing a plan can make the health insurance process much easier.
