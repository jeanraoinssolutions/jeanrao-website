What Is ACA / Obamacare?

The Affordable Care Act (ACA), often called "Obamacare," created the Health Insurance Marketplace where eligible individuals and families can shop for health insurance coverage.

Depending on your household circumstances, income, and other eligibility factors, you may qualify for financial assistance that can reduce the cost of Marketplace coverage.

Understanding the basic rules before choosing a plan can make the health insurance process much easier.

COMPARE YOUR OPTIONS

Which Health Insurance Option Fits Your Situation?

ACA Marketplace coverage is an important option, but it may not be the only option available to you.

ACA Marketplace

Individual and family health insurance available through the Marketplace. Eligible applicants may qualify for financial assistance.

Employer Coverage

Employer-sponsored health insurance may be an option when coverage is available and meets applicable requirements.

COBRA

After losing employer coverage, some people may consider continuing their existing employer health plan through COBRA.

Other Private Health Insurance

Certain private health insurance options exist outside the ACA Marketplace. Eligibility, benefits, costs, and limitations vary by plan.

BEFORE MEDICARE

Health Insurance Before Age 65

People who retire or leave an employer before becoming eligible for Medicare may need to find individual health insurance coverage.

ACA Marketplace coverage may be one option to consider. Depending on your circumstances, other coverage options may also be worth investigating.
