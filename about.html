MY BACKGROUND

An Analytical Approach to Insurance

My professional background is in engineering, where problem-solving, careful analysis, and attention to detail are part of my everyday work.

I bring the same approach to insurance. Rather than simply presenting a policy, I believe it is important to understand a family's situation first and then explain the available options clearly.

Insurance can involve unfamiliar terminology, complicated choices, and significant financial decisions. My goal is to make the process easier to understand.

WHY I BECAME AN AGENT

Insurance Became Personal for Me

My interest in insurance grew from my own experiences as a working professional and a member of a family making important financial decisions.

Employment changes can happen unexpectedly, and when employer-sponsored benefits change or disappear, families may suddenly need to reconsider their insurance coverage.

I have experienced these changes personally. I also understand the frustration of reviewing auto and home insurance when premiums increase even though your circumstances and claims history may not have changed.

These experiences made me realize how valuable it can be to have someone who is willing to listen, explain the choices, and take the time to help compare available options.

MY APPROACH
