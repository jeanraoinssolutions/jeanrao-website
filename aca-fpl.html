Federal Poverty Level (FPL) Guidelines for Texas

HealthCare.gov uses annual Federal Poverty Level (FPL) income thresholds to determine your eligibility for Marketplace premium subsidies (APTC) and Cost-Sharing Reductions (CSR).

Important: These are approximate reference figures for the 2026 federal poverty guidelines for the 48 contiguous states and D.C. (which includes Texas). Actual Marketplace financial assistance depends on the applicable coverage year, household size, estimated household income, tax household, state, and other eligibility requirements. Alaska and Hawaii have different FPL amounts. This table is for general educational purposes and is not an eligibility determination.

Texas Specific Rules & Timing Details

Texas Medicaid Gap (Under 100% FPL)

Because Texas has not expanded Medicaid, non-disabled adults earning under 100% FPL generally do not qualify for Medicaid or Marketplace subsidies. Marketplace subsidy eligibility in Texas begins at 100% FPL ($15,960 for an individual in 2026).

How FPL Timing Works for Plan Years

For Premium Tax Credit purposes, the applicable FPL guideline is based on the most recently published FPL figures available on the first day of the annual Open Enrollment Period. For example, Open Enrollment for the 2026 plan year relies on the 2025 HHS poverty guidelines. When preparing for upcoming plan years, we ensure calculations use the exact federally mandated guidelines for that specific enrollment cycle.
