Federal Poverty Level (FPL) Guidelines

The Federal Poverty Level (FPL) is an annual income guideline used by HealthCare.gov to determine your eligibility for Marketplace subsidies and Cost-Sharing Reductions.

How FPL Determines Your ACA Savings

Your subsidy level depends on where your household Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) falls relative to the FPL percentage tiers:

  • Below 100% – 138% FPL: May qualify for Medicaid (in expansion states) or Marketplace subsidies depending on state laws.
  • 100% – 250% FPL: Qualifies for maximum Premium Tax Credits and extra Cost-Sharing Reductions (CSR) when enrolling in a Silver plan.
  • 250% – 400% FPL: Qualifies for Premium Tax Credits to lower monthly premiums.
  • Above 400% FPL: Generally full price; subsidies fade out depending on current federal tax laws.

How to Calculate Your Household FPL Percentage

Divide your projected annual household MAGI by the 100% FPL figure for your household size, then multiply by 100.

Example: A family of 3 with an estimated income of $53,300 calculates: ($53,300 ÷ $26,650) × 100 = 200% FPL.
